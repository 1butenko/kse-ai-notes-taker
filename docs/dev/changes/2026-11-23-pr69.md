# Refuse SVG figures whose labels are crossed by lines

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **PR:** #69
- **Branch:** fix/svg-label-check → main

## What changed
- `render_notes` now refuses a hand-made SVG figure when a line, curve, axis, guide, shape edge, moving element or another label runs through a `<text>` label.
- Label boxes are estimated from the font size (0.55 em per character) and shrunk by 1.5 units, so a graze passes. Paths are sampled (cubic and quadratic curves, arcs) and transforms are applied.
- Animated elements are checked along their way: SMIL `animate` on geometry (frames paired when timing matches), `animateTransform` translate, `animateMotion`/`mpath`, and CSS `@keyframes` translations.
- Exempt: labels with a halo (`stroke` + `paint-order="stroke"`), rotated text, and labels inside a box (only edges count).
- The check runs from `validate.svg_issues`, and only when the other SVG checks found no problems.
- The `svg-figures` skill tells the writer about the check.

## Why
Generated hook figures had labels on top of their curves and a y-axis through the slider labels. Prompt rules alone did not stop it.
